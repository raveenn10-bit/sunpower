06 / A LITTLE TRANSPARENCY

Privacy &
website terms.

What this website does—and what it doesn’t.

Your information

This website does not create customer accounts, accept payments, or store contact forms on a server. Your inquiry list is saved in this browser using local storage so it can stay with you between visits. You can clear it using “Clear list”. If browser storage is unavailable, the list works for your current visit.

WhatsApp inquiries

When you choose to continue on WhatsApp, your selected items and any contact form details are included in a prefilled WhatsApp message. You review and send that message yourself. Opening the message does not mean an order has been placed. WhatsApp and Meta handle that interaction under their own policies.

External services

Map directions open Google Maps. Social links open Facebook or Instagram. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection data such as an IP address. This site has no added advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Catalogue and images

The catalogue is a selection guide, not a live stock feed. Photography is illustrative and does not show actual Sunpower stores or exact branded products. Brands, stock, sizes, prices and store information should be confirmed directly with the team.

Orders, collection and delivery

All inquiries require store confirmation. No delivery coverage, delivery fees, payment methods or return terms are promised by this website. Agree those details with the store before placing an order or paying.

Contact

For website or privacy questions, contact Sunpower on 077 677 5956.
