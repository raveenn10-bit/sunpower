A FRESH PERSPECTIVE

Small discoveries.

Sunpower Farmers Market brings food and natural product selections together on Sri Lanka’s southern coast, with locations in Dewata, Galle Fort and Ahangama.

From Miss NuTri granola and Nourish yogurt to selections from Momiji Natural and Ceylon Coconut Company, our market is a place to discover something for your daily routine.

Visit a store to see the latest range, or send a shopping inquiry to our team.
